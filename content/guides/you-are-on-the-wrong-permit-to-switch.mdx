## The category someone is on can matter more than the case they have

A strong job offer, a genuine relationship, or a real course place is sometimes not the deciding factor at all. Whether an application can even be made from inside the country depends first on which category the applicant currently holds — and on at least one route this site covers, one specific starting category is reported as an almost total bar, regardless of how strong the new case is.

## The visitor route: reported as close to an absolute bar

Someone on a UK Standard Visitor visa is reported generally unable to switch to any other visa category from inside the UK, and this is described as a broad prohibition — even a genuine job offer or a marriage to a British citizen does not create an exception in most cases. The applicant is reported required to leave the UK and apply for entry clearance from outside, from their country of nationality or usual residence, rather than switch in-country. The stated reasoning is that visitor permission is treated as temporary permission for limited, specific activities, not as a general stepping stone toward work, study or settlement — and an application to switch from a category where switching is not permitted is reported leading to a mandatory refusal, not merely a harder assessment.

## Why this catches genuinely strong cases specifically

This restriction does not filter for weak cases — it applies regardless of the new application's own merits. An applicant who arrived as a visitor and, entirely genuinely, received a job offer or began a real relationship during that visit is still caught by the same bar as someone with a weaker case, because the restriction is about the starting category, not the ending one. This is precisely the scenario where the strength of the new case can create false confidence: a compelling job offer feels like it should count for something, and under this specific rule it generally does not.

## What actually determines whether switching is possible

The category currently held is the first thing to check, before any assessment of whether the new route's own requirements are met. Some categories are reported permitting in-country switching to specific other routes; the visitor category is reported as one of the categories that generally does not, for almost any destination category. This is not a judgement call an applicant can improve their odds on by building a stronger application — where switching is not permitted from a specific starting category, no quality of supporting evidence changes that outcome.

## What to actually do if switching from a visitor category

Where the route being sought is one visitor status cannot switch into, plan on leaving to apply from outside the country rather than assembling evidence for an in-country switch that faces a mandatory refusal regardless of its strength. This also means the timing of accepting a job offer or beginning any new immigration plan, relative to entering on a visitor visa in the first place, is worth thinking about deliberately — entering under a different category from the outset, where realistic, avoids this restriction entirely rather than requiring a workaround for it later.
