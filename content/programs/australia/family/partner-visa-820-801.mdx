## What this route actually is

The onshore Partner visa is really two visas bundled into one application: a temporary stage (820) and a permanent stage (801), assessed in sequence but applied for and paid for together. An applicant already in Australia submits one combined application; approval of the 820 stage grants temporary status while the 801 stage — which by law cannot even be assessed until two years have passed since lodgement — works through in the background.

That structure is worth understanding before anything else, because it sets real expectations about timeline. This is not a route that resolves in months. Between the 820 stage's own reported processing and the mandatory two-year floor before the 801 stage can be decided, a realistic total timeline commonly runs to three years or longer.

## Who this suits, and who it does not

It suits a couple in a genuine, well-documented relationship, already living in Australia together, prepared for a genuinely multi-year process and a combined application charge that has recently run above AUD 11,000. It suits it less well a couple early in a relationship with limited shared evidence — the assessment leans on documented history across several dimensions of the relationship, not on the relationship's sincerity alone.

It is also specifically an onshore route. An applicant outside Australia at the time of application needs the separate offshore pathway (subclass 309/100), which this record does not cover and which has its own process and timeline.

## What the evidence actually needs to show

Relationship evidence for this route is commonly assessed across four dimensions: the financial aspects of the relationship, the nature of the shared household, social recognition of the relationship by others, and the nature of the couple's commitment to each other. A strong case usually has to show something in each category rather than an overwhelming amount in just one — a couple with combined finances but almost no evidence that friends or family recognise the relationship can still present as under-evidenced.
