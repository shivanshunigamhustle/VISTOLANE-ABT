## What this route actually is

The subclass 500 is Australia's single visa for international students, covering everyone from a short English-language course through to a full degree, provided the course is on CRICOS, the government's register of courses approved for international students. The application is built around one document above all others, the Confirmation of Enrolment, but eligibility runs on more than enrolment alone.

The central test is the Genuine Student requirement, which replaced the older Genuine Temporary Entrant test on 23 March 2025. That change matters for anyone reading older guidance on this route: the assessment now runs against six specific factors, study commitment, compliance history, financial circumstances, immigration history, personal circumstances and ties to the home country, rather than the framework it replaced.

## Who this suits, and who it does not

It suits a genuine student with a real offer, a course that is actually on CRICOS, and financial evidence that clearly supports the specific study plan. It suits it less well an applicant using enrolment mainly as a route toward something else; the Genuine Student requirement is built specifically to weigh that possibility, and a thin or generic case is one of the more common reasons an otherwise eligible application does not succeed.

The financial bar here is real and specific: recently reported living-costs figures run well into five figures for the primary applicant alone, before course fees, health cover, and additional amounts for any accompanying family are added. This is not a route to plan around minimum viable evidence.

## Where this page is incomplete

Several fields are marked unverified rather than stated as settled: current work-hour rules for student visa holders (which have changed materially in recent years), the exact typical visa grant length and any post-study allowance, current English test score thresholds and exemptions, and current rules on including family members. The fee and living-cost figures quoted here are recent reported figures rather than figures independently confirmed against the Department's live fee schedule, and both are revised periodically; they should be checked directly before being used to set an applicant's budget.
