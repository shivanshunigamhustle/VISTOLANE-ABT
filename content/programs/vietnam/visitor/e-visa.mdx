## What this route actually is

Vietnam's e-visa is the government's own direct online visa channel, run through the National Immigration Department's official portal; applied for, paid for and approved without going through an embassy or consulate in person. It covers tourism and short business trips for up to 90 days, with single- or multiple-entry options, and is the route most visitors without visa-exemption use.

The most consequential detail sits at the edges of the process rather than in the application form itself: the e-visa is only valid for entry and exit through a defined list of approved checkpoints, not any airport or land border in the country.

## Who this suits, and who it does not

It suits a traveler from an eligible nationality planning to enter and exit through approved checkpoints, applying directly at the official government portal rather than a commercial intermediary. It suits it less well someone whose nationality already qualifies for visa-exemption; applying for and paying for an e-visa in that case is an avoidable, non-refundable cost for something the applicant did not need.

## Apply at the official portal, not a reseller

A large number of commercial websites offer to "process" the same e-visa application for a fee well above the government's own charge. Since the actual application, photo upload, passport scan, payment, happens on the government's own site regardless, applying there directly avoids that markup entirely and reduces the number of parties handling the applicant's passport information.

## Where this page is incomplete

Several fields are marked unverified rather than stated as settled: the current full list of eligible nationalities and approved entry/exit checkpoints (both of which the Vietnamese government adjusts from time to time), the current minimum passport validity requirement, current rules on extending or converting an e-visa from within Vietnam, and the current list of nationalities and stay lengths covered by visa-exemption. These should be confirmed at evisa.gov.vn before a specific applicant relies on them.
