## What this route actually is

Vietnam's DT visa ties residence status directly to the size of a foreign investor's capital contribution to a Vietnamese company, split into four tiers, DT1 through DT4, each with materially different outcomes. The tier is not just a label: it sets both the initial visa's validity and whether the investor can later convert to a temporary residence card at all. DT1 and DT2, the largest tiers, are reported leading to residence cards of up to 10 and 5 years respectively; DT4, the smallest, is reported as having no card option, leaving the investor renewing a 12-month visa year after year.

That gap between tiers is large enough to be a genuine planning consideration, not a footnote; an investment structured without checking which tier it produces can land an investor in a meaningfully less convenient position than a slightly larger, differently structured investment would have.

## Who this suits, and who it does not

It suits an investor with a genuine, properly documented capital stake in a Vietnamese company, supported by the company's Enterprise Registration Certificate and, where applicable, an Investment Registration Certificate, who has checked which DT tier the investment size actually produces before finalizing the structure. It suits it less well an investor expecting residence status roughly proportional to any investment size without checking the specific thresholds; the difference between just above and just below a tier boundary can be the difference between a multi-year residence card and an annual visa renewal cycle.

## The residence card, where available, is the real prize

For DT1 through DT3, the visa itself is only the entry point. The temporary residence card that follows, applied for separately after arrival and address registration, is what actually functions as a long-term residence and re-entry permit, removing the need to renew a visa repeatedly. Treating the initial visa as the end goal, rather than a step toward the card, understates what the larger tiers on this route are actually capable of delivering.
