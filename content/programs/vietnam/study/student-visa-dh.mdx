## What this route actually is

The DH visa is Vietnam's dedicated category for foreign students, distinct from the short-stay e-visa or tourist exemptions that cover ordinary visits. It is built around a sponsoring Vietnamese educational institution's admission letter, and in practice the school itself is commonly involved in processing the visa, not just issuing the paperwork that starts it.

That institutional involvement is worth planning around directly; coordinating early with a prospective school's international office, rather than treating the visa as a separate task to handle independently once admission is confirmed, tends to make the process considerably smoother.

## Who this suits, and who it does not

It suits a genuinely admitted student with a confirmed acceptance letter, applying through the correct study-specific category rather than a tourist e-visa that happens to cover a similar length of stay. It suits it less well anyone hoping to enter on a tourist visa and study informally; the DH visa exists specifically because a short-stay category is not the right instrument for a genuine study stay, and using the wrong category creates avoidable status risk.

## The visa is not the only formality

Arrival in Vietnam on a valid DH visa is not the end of the process. Foreign students are generally expected to register their residential address with local police shortly after arriving; a separate administrative step from the visa itself, easy to overlook if the visa is treated as the finish line rather than one part of a small sequence of requirements.
