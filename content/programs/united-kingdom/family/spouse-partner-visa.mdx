## What this route actually is

The UK's spouse and partner visa is built around three separate requirements that all have to hold at once: a genuine relationship, a sponsor who clears a specific minimum income or savings threshold, and adequate accommodation. Of the three, the financial requirement is the one most likely to surprise an otherwise straightforward, genuine couple — it is assessed against defined evidence categories, not a general sense of the couple's financial comfort, and it has moved substantially in recent years.

This is also, in most cases, only the first stage of a longer route. The initial visa is typically time-limited, with a further period and further requirements standing between it and permanent settlement — a structure this record deliberately does not describe in full detail without confirming the current specifics, since getting the multi-stage timeline wrong would understate what a couple is actually signing up for.

## Who this suits, and who it does not

It suits a couple in a genuine, well-documented relationship where the UK-based sponsor can evidence income or savings against the current threshold using accepted evidence types — payslips, an employment letter, tax returns, or qualifying savings. It suits it less well a couple where the sponsor's income is real but difficult to evidence in the specific accepted formats, or where an unmarried partnership has not yet reached the 2-year threshold that lets it be assessed on the same basis as a marriage.

## The financial requirement is not about the couple's actual finances

This is worth stating plainly: the requirement is not an assessment of whether the couple can support themselves in some general sense. It is a specific, defined threshold checked against specific, defined categories of evidence. A sponsor with substantial but hard-to-document income — irregular self-employment income without clean records, for instance — can fail this requirement even where the couple's actual financial position is comfortable. Getting the evidence into the right format matters as much as the income existing at all.
