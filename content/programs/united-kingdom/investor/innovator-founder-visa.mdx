## What this route actually is

The Innovator Founder visa is the UK's route for entrepreneurs, and, much like Canada's Start-up Visa on this site, its real gatekeeper is not the Home Office at all, at least not first. Before a visa application can be submitted, the applicant has to secure endorsement from a Home Office approved endorsing body: a commercial organisation that independently assesses whether the business is genuinely new, innovative, viable and scalable. Only once that endorsement exists does the immigration process itself really begin.

That structure means the practical difficulty of this route sits mostly upstream of the visa application. An applicant with a weak or generic business plan is unlikely to be endorsed at all, regardless of how straightforward the rest of the visa process might otherwise be.

## Who this suits, and who it does not

It suits a founder with a genuinely developed, specific business plan capable of persuading a commercial endorsing body it is worth backing, and who is prepared for meaningfully more ongoing cost and process than a simple one-off visa fee suggests, endorsement fees, periodic contact point meeting charges through the life of the visa, the visa fee itself, and the Immigration Health Surcharge all add up. It suits it less well anyone hoping capital alone, without a strong business case, will carry the application, this route does not run on a capital threshold, and available money without a business an endorsing body will back does not qualify.

## The endorsement relationship does not end at approval

Unlike a one-time eligibility check, endorsement on this route comes with an ongoing relationship: endorsed applicants are generally expected to attend periodic contact point meetings with their endorsing body through the visa period, demonstrating continued progress against the original business plan. That is a meaningfully different commitment from a route where the assessment happens once and the applicant is otherwise left alone, worth factoring into any realistic view of what this visa actually involves after it is granted.
