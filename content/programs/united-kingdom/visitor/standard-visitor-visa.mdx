## What this route actually is

The Standard Visitor visa is the UK's general-purpose short-stay route, covering tourism, seeing family and friends, and a defined range of business activities, but not employment. Processing is reported to formally begin from the date of the applicant's biometric appointment at a visa application centre, not from when the online form is submitted, which matters for anyone planning around a specific travel date.

Standard processing has recently run around 3 weeks outside the UK, but that figure moves with the season; peak periods such as summer or the December holidays have been reported pushing it out to 5 or 6 weeks. Faster paid services exist in some countries for applicants who need more certainty on timing.

## Who this suits, and who it does not

It suits a genuine visitor with clear evidence of funds and of intent to leave at the end of the stay, planning with realistic lead time around the biometric appointment rather than the online submission date alone. It does not suit anyone hoping to work in the UK; the boundary between permitted business activity and prohibited employment is specific, and this route grants no general right to work.

## Where the real cost sits

The headline visa fee is only part of the picture once accommodation, travel and any priority processing service are added, and the visa fee itself was reported increasing on 8 April 2026, which is a reminder that these figures move and should be checked close to the time of application rather than relied on from older guidance.
